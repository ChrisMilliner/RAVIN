# Grounded Overview Evidence Audit

## Purpose

This document records the independent evidence audit used to define
required evidence groups for questions classified as
`GROUNDED_OVERVIEW`.

Evidence groups are defined before overview retrieval scores are
calculated. This prevents retrieval results from influencing the
benchmark definition.

Within an evidence group, multiple evidence targets may represent
acceptable alternatives.

Between required evidence groups, all groups must be covered for the
overview question to achieve complete evidence coverage.

---

## RB002

### Question

What happens when a student is not making satisfactory academic
progress?

### Behaviour

`GROUNDED_OVERVIEW`

### Audit decision

A satisfactory overview should explain the staged academic progression
process, identify that support and interventions form part of that
process, and explain that continued progression difficulties may
escalate to show cause and possible course exclusion.

Three required evidence groups are therefore defined.

### Required group 1 - progression_stages

**Concept**

Subject failure triggers a staged academic progression process.

**Required meaning**

The evidence must establish that subject failure can trigger one of
three academic progression stages:

- AP1 - Recovery
- AP2 - Support and Sanction
- AP3 - Show Cause and Course Exclusion

**Source**

Policy 220 - Academic Progression Review Policy

Heading:

`Section 6 - Procedures > Part A - Monitoring and Determining Academic Progression`

Stable answer-bearing fragment:

`Students who experience subject failure will trigger one of three stages of academic progression`

### Required group 2 - support_and_interventions

**Concept**

Academic progression management includes support and interventions.

**Required meaning**

The evidence must establish that students subject to progression
monitoring receive or may receive support/interventions as part of the
staged process.

**Acceptable source alternative A**

Policy 220 - Academic Progression Review Policy

Heading:

`Section 5 - Policy Statement`

Stable answer-bearing fragment:

`interventions at each stage of academic progression monitoring provide students with multiple avenues for academic and non-academic support`

**Acceptable source alternative B**

Policy 220 - Academic Progression Review Policy

Heading:

`Section 6 - Procedures > Part A - Monitoring and Determining Academic Progression`

Stable answer-bearing fragment:

`associated support and interventions`

### Required group 3 - escalation_and_consequences

**Concept**

Continued progression difficulties may escalate to show cause and
possible course exclusion.

**Required meaning**

The evidence must establish that the progression process can reach AP3
and involve show cause and potential exclusion from the course.

**Acceptable source alternative A**

Policy 220 - Academic Progression Review Policy

Heading:

`Section 6 - Procedures > Part A - Monitoring and Determining Academic Progression`

Stable answer-bearing fragment:

`Show Cause and Course Exclusion`

**Acceptable source alternative B**

Policy 220 - Academic Progression Review Policy

Heading:

`Section 6 - Procedures > Part B - Details of Academic Progression Stages > Academic Progression Stage Three - Show Cause and Course Exclusion > Show Cause`

Stable answer-bearing fragment:

`show cause as to why they should not be excluded from their course`

**Acceptable source alternative C**

Policy 220 - Academic Progression Review Policy

Heading:

`Section 6 - Procedures > Part B - Details of Academic Progression Stages > Academic Progression Stage Three - Show Cause and Course Exclusion > Show Cause`

Stable answer-bearing fragment:

`University will take steps to exclude them from their course`

### Supplementary but not required evidence

The following information may improve an answer but is not required for
RB002 to achieve complete overview coverage:

- review of an exclusion decision through the University Appeals
  Committee;
- reapplication or admission to another course following exclusion;
- international-student CoE or visa consequences;
- course-transfer consequences.

These details are narrower follow-on matters and are not necessary to
answer the general question about what happens when satisfactory
academic progress is not being made.

### Audit outcome

Required evidence groups: **3**

- `progression_stages`
- `support_and_interventions`
- `escalation_and_consequences`

RB002 should pass Grounded Overview evidence coverage only when all
three required groups are covered.
